Harmonic Fusion

Our mission is to nurture a vibrant community who dance, play, connect, care, and love. We create harmony between differences and enrich the fusion dance scene in Eugene through joyful, heart-led gatherings.

7–10pm every Thursday at the Veteran's Memorial Club.

Learn with Dylan

Dylan explores partner styles from Lindy Hop to blues to tango and contact improv, and teaches fusion built around connection and presence. Open to travel for teaching and DJ sets.
